charts and the problem is that at some point, the non-elliptical wing WILL

There's a few things here.  The CG should never be in front of the CL unless
there's some kind of ordnance or fuel or something that will move it back to a
critical location.  The CG should always be between the 2 flying surfaces
(wing and tail), or at the least on the CG.  The CG is usually behind the Lift
to counter the negative pitching moment, so that the tail doesn't have to do
it, causing trim drag.

The angle of attack capability (induced angle of attack) is also related to
the aspect ratio.  The formula is

(18.24 x Cl)/AR

A tail with a low aspect ratio relative to the wing would have a slightly
higher angle of attack than the 2-dimensional plot might suggest.  Same for a
plane with a lower wing aspect ratio.  For example, say the plot shows a max
lift of 1.5 at 15 degrees AoA.  The corrected value with aspect ratio = 5.5
would be 1.5 @ 20 degrees.

You can do from -90 to 90 and play with control surface deflections and
