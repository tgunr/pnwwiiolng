Benefits of WingSections
Airfoil Analysis WingSections allows you to analyze airfoils and compare their performance. This is important because once you have chosen a wing size, aspect ratio and other dimensions, the only other way to improve the performance is through the airfoil. The airfoil determines the lift and the form drag at various angles of attack . What determines the good performing airfoils? Some conditions are the extent of laminar flow along the surface of the airfoils, surface pressure gradients, airfoil thickness and camber.  If you are comparing pre-built airplanes, then compare the airfoils sections. WingSections can help to determine the best performer.
Airfoil Plotting Most analysis tools do not provide this function. WingSections allows you to complete your project by providing you with a set of linearly tapered templates for constructing a wing.  
Exporting to DXF Files If you are using CAD software to design your airplane, WingSections allows you to export your airfoil section into the software via the DXF file format.
Cost Shop around and you will find that WingSections provides you with airfoil data at the lowest price given all of its functions and capabilities.   WingSections combines airfoil analysis, airfoil plotting, exporting to DXF files (CAD capabilities) for less than most analysis tools.
