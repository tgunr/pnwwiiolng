Benefits of VisualFoil

Modify Your Custom or Library Airfoils

Interactively modify airfoil thickness and camber at any angle of attack and observe the motion of the stagnation and separation points.

Estimate Forces on Your Wing.
VisualFoil 4.0 allows you to estimate the lift, drag, moment and other dimensional forces on your wing. It also computes the Reynolds number for your airfoil.
Compute the Aerodynamics Coefficients for your Airfoils.
Use the accurate aerodynamics analysis to find the lift force, drag force and moments on your airfoil. These coefficients are necessary to fix the weight, engine size and stability of your aircraft.Use the large airfoil library to select airfoils for your wing, propeller, hull, helicopter blade, race car spoiler or other aerodynamics project. No longer spend hours searching for the best airfoil.  Use Visualfoil to find it and get its aerodynamics characteristics in minutes.

Compare the Performance of Your Airfoils
Use the polar plot to determine the operations limits of your airfoil. The plots show which airfoil has the best drag characteristics over a range of angles of attack.  Knowing the total drag on your airplane will help you to select the best engine and determine the sink rate or rate of climb during operations. 


Test the Lifting Ability of Airfoils

Use the Lift Coefficient vs Angle of Attack plot to determine which airfoil provides optimum maneuvering capabilities.  The diagram shows maximum lift and the angle for maximum lift.  These numbers help you to determine your stall speeds as well as operating limits of your airplane.  You can also use these numbers to set your geometric angle of attack.  .

Compute Surface Airfoil Conditions
Use VisualFoil to compute and plot the pressure coefficient and velocity ratio distribution on the surface of your airfoil.
Use the streamlines and pressure coefficient plots for education. Show students the various flow fields that can develop as the angle of attack is varied or different airfoil configurations are selected. You can used the unlimited zooming capabilities to study features of interest.

O-Grid Generation
Use the built-in O-Grid generator to quickly generate a grid for any airfoil.  I use this feature to generate grids for my Navier-Stokes analysis.  The CFD programs solves the equations on each grid point and provides a detailed description of the flow.
