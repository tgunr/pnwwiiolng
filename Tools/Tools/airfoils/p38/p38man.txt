Some points of interest

Stall speeds: (15000 lbs), 94 mph gear and flaps up, 69 mph gear and flaps
down
17000 lbs :  100 mph G&F up, 74 mph down
19000 lbs:  105 mph up, 78 mph down

AIRSPEED LIMITATIONS:

Landing gear down:  175 mph IAS
Flaps 100% extended:  150 mph
Flaps 50% extended:  250 mph
Landing light extended:  140 mph

Dangerous instability exists when the center of gravity is aft of 32% mac.
(32% gear up corresponds to 28.5% gear down).  Under these tail heavy
conditions, full down elevator will be required to prevent stalling the
airplane if the airspeed is allowed to drop below 90 mph IAS with flaps down,
power on, and gear up.  Tail heavy conditions may be relieved by lowering the
landing gear.

Fuel consumptions are as follows (per engine):

3000rpm, 54 inches MP, 1425hp, 167 US gal/hr, critical altitude 22000' without
ram, 24900' with ram, 15 minute limit

3000rpm, 60 ", 1600 hp, 180 US gal/hr, critical alitudes 7000' without ram,
10000' with ram, 5 minute limit

2600 rpm, 44", 1100 hp, 113 US gal/hr, 31600' without ram, 34000' with ram

2300 rpm, 35", 795 hp, 63 US gal/hr, 36700' without ram, 38000' with ram
