6.5.1   How can I open the Library folder?

Under Mac OS X 10.7 Lion or later, the Library folder is hidden. To open it, click on Finder in the Dock. Click on the Go menu at the top of the screen, hold down the Option key, and then choose Library.

Under previous versions of Mac OS X, you can click on Finder in the Dock, click on the Go menu, and choose Home. This will show your home folder, and you can open the Library folder directly.
