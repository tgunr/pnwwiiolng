6.4   Why doesn’t my serial number work?

You need to enter the serial name and serial number exactly as they appear on your order confirmation e-mail. If you no longer have this e-mail, you can look up the name and number. Launch DropDMG and choose Purchase from the DropDMG menu. Then click the Look Up My Serial Number button.
